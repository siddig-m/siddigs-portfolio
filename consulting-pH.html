The Architecture

In this project, we delve into the factors influencing mercury levels in fish across Maine's lakes. We address three crucial research questions using data from 120 lakes in the state.

By employing statistical methods such as one-sample t-tests, two-sided t-tests, and ANOVA tests, we provide valuable insights into mercury levels in Maine's fish populations.

These findings can guide policy decisions and actions to preserve the health of Maine's lakes and their aquatic ecosystems.

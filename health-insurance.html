The Architecture

This project delves into the complex world of insurance pricing, particularly in the context of health insurance. It explores how various factors impact insurance costs and aims to predict future charges with precision.

The team analyzed a dataset featuring health information of 1,338 medical patients and their corresponding insurance charges. Through a comprehensive examination of the data, we sought to uncover significant insights both visually and statistically.

The ultimate goal was to develop a multiple linear regression model capable of forecasting insurance costs based on key variables (such as age, smoking habits, and family size), potentially offering valuable insights for insurance companies and policyholders alike.
